# Blip — Awake b

Approved identity, version 0.3.1. Concept 1 combines a lowercase b with two negative-space eyes. Preserve its compound-path geometry.

## Palette
- Mark: graphite `#111116`.
- Application tile: warm paper `#f3f1eb`.
- Reversed standalone mark: white `#ffffff`.
- No cyan eyes, gradients, glow, shadows or dark app-icon tile.

## Production files
- `blip-symbol.svg`: transparent monochrome master.
- `blip-symbol-small.svg`: same legible geometry for small sizes.
- `blip-symbol-color.svg`: monochrome identity; historical filename retained for asset consumers.
- `blip-lockup-horizontal.svg`: symbol with existing outlined wordmark, monochrome.
- `app-icon.svg`: light rounded tile and graphite mark.
- `../icon.ico`: Windows executable, tray and installer resource; 16/24/32/48/64/128/256 px.
- `icon.ico`: identical branding export.
- `variants/`: matching SVG, PNG and favicon exports. Legacy filename `blip-symbol-mono-4cc9f0.svg` now contains graphite, not cyan.
- `exploration-v03/`: historical concepts, not production assets.

## Usage
Keep proportions and eye cutouts. Minimum size 16 px. Leave master viewBox spacing intact. Use reversed standalone artwork on dark surfaces, or the light application tile on any wallpaper. Do not recolor eyes separately or restore the previous glowing capsule identity. Notch UI colors are separate from application-icon identity.
